SCANS

ACHTEROOM CORNER GAUSSIAN SPLAT

This render is based on a photogrammetry scan of Achterom Street, Eindhoven, created from 106 images. The scan was processed using COLMAP and trained with gsplat on Linux, resulting in a complete model after approximately 1.5 hours of processing.

EINDHOVEN CENTER CHURCH GAUSSIAN SPLAT

Photogrammetry scan of St. Catherine’s Church, Eindhoven, created from 20 images using COLMAP and gsplat, with a total processing time of 45 minutes.

ACHTEROOM CORNER MESH

Mesh-based photogrammetry model created from 106 images using Meshroom, with high-density SIFT descriptors and optimized reconstruction settings. Total processing time: 7h 30m.

EINDHOVEN CENTER CHURCH MESH

Mesh-based photogrammetry model of St. Catherine’s Church, Eindhoven, created from 20 images in Meshroom with high-quality SIFT descriptors and 8K textures. Processing time: 4 hours.

EFFENAAR MESH

Mesh-based photogrammetry model of Effenaar, Eindhoven, created from 112 images in Meshroom with 8K textures. Processing time: 8+ hours.
